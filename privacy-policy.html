Privacy policy

Last updated:

Jul 16, 2026

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. this privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or use our services.

Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and other details you provide through forms.

  • Usage data: Information about how you interact with our website, such as pages visited, time spent, and clicks.

  • Device information: Browser type, device type, ip address, and operating system.

How we use your information

we use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide support

  • Manage and improve our services

  • Personalize your experience

  • Send updates, offers, or marketing communications (only if you opt in)

  • Analyze website performance and user behavior

sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. we may share your information with:

  • Trusted service providers (e.g., hosting, analytics, email tools)

  • Legal authorities if required by law

  • Partners only when necessary to deliver services

data security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. however, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data

  • Request correction or deletion

  • Withdraw consent for marketing

  • Request data portability

Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. we are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
